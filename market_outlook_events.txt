CRUELTY SQUAD ONLINE: RECONSTRUCTED - MARKET OUTLOOK EVENTS

Each listed mission has the authored stock impacts shown below. They begin
after the first successful completion in a save slot and unfold over 20 seconds.
Ordinary market movement continues at the same time, so the observed price
change may differ from the listed percentage. Replays do not repeat an event.
Pre-mission outlooks describe the forecast without exact percentages.
The latest completed mission's authored impacts appear beside the Market
Outlook heading only while the 20-second impact is active.
Secret mission forecasts appear only after those missions are unlocked.
The Headquarters forecast appears only after Hope Eradicated and Death mode
have both been active together at least once in that save slot.
GAGA's -100% Apartment Atrocity event takes its price to zero over 20 seconds;
it cannot be bought while bankrupt. Archon Grid restores it at its original
listing price before applying the market-wide rise.
