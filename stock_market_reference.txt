CRUELTY SQUAD STOCK MARKET — IMPLEMENTATION REFERENCE
CruS Online: RECONSTRUCTED, 8 October 2026

Scope and source
----------------
This describes the stock market as implemented in the current project. Market
data and price simulation are in remaped/Stock_Handler.gd. The trading screen
uses remaped/Stocks.gd, based on the original game's Stocks.gd.
Physical body-part pickups use remaped/Asset_Pickup.gd; fishing uses the base
game's Scripts/Fishing_Hook.gd. Save-slot routing is in remaped/Game_Manager.gd.
The catalogue at the end is generated from the current Stock_Handler.gd data.

What is traded
--------------
There are 103 assets: 12 company equities, 18 body parts, and 73 fish. Every
asset has a unique four-character ticker (some include a trailing space), a
name, description, current price, amount owned, trend, volatility, nominal
minimum/maximum prices, and an issued-share count. All three tabs use the same
buy/sell actions, but company shares now have their own price simulation.
Body parts and fish are not a separate currency: their market holdings are
valued and sold like shares.

The Stocks, Parts, and Fish tabs list their assets by ticker. Each row shows
the owned quantity, price change since the last market tick, and, if any are
owned, the rounded current value of that holding. An upward or unchanged tick
is tinted pale green; a downward tick is tinted pale red. The panel also shows
cash and the sum of current price times owned quantity for every asset as
"Total holdings". That figure does not include cash.

Selecting an asset shows its description, name, owned amount, current price,
percentage change from its fixed starting price, and "MKC" (current price
times issued shares). The graph plots the most recent 100 simulated prices;
its segments are green when rising and red when falling. That graph history is
not saved. The market panel refreshes its text every fifth physics frame while
visible, whereas prices tick on their own timer even when the panel is closed.
The stock list is shorter to make room for a Market Outlook panel beneath it.

Trading
-------
Buy and Sell each have buttons for 1, 2, 5, 10, and 100 units. A buy succeeds
only when cash is STRICTLY GREATER than current price times quantity, and the
unit price is greater than $0.90. Consequently, having exactly the displayed
cost is insufficient. A successful buy subtracts that cost from cash and adds
the quantity to the holding. A sale requires at least that many units, removes
them, and adds current price times quantity to cash. There is no transaction
fee, spread, slippage, short selling, or share-availability check in this code.
Cash and stock data are saved after successful trades.

Undiscovered body parts and fish display a not-found icon, and their name and
description appear as "???" in the detail panel. Their tickers, prices, and
trading buttons remain available. Buying one does not count as finding it.
Discovery is recorded separately when a body-part pickup is used or a hooked
fish is actually returned to the player. Found parts use their name in the
discovery save list; found fish use their ticker. The corresponding model can
then appear in the market preview. Picking up a body part also adds one owned
unit; catching a fish adds one owned unit.

Price updates
-------------
The stock handler starts a repeating 1-second Timer. With Eyes of Corporate
Insight equipped, its wait_time is 0.1 seconds. Every asset updates while the
game runs, even when the market panel is closed.

Company equities use a percentage-based model. Each company spends about
90-240 simulated seconds in a calm, growing, declining, or crashing state,
then randomly changes state. The respective per-second drifts are +0.005%,
+0.12%, -0.06%, and -0.2%, with Gaussian noise scaled by company volatility
and elapsed time. A company below 12% of its starting value enters growth
on its next state change. New company prices are bounded between 3% and
2,000% of starting price, with a $0.90 absolute floor; legacy saved prices
above that ceiling are preserved rather than abruptly clipped. Faster ticks
from Eyes of Corporate Insight do not accelerate company-price movement.

Fish and body parts keep the original additive rules. Their effective trend
starts at their stored trend. LEVELS_UNLOCKED exactly 6 adds 0.1; reaching
price_action_day adds price_action while the current price is strictly within
its nominal range; matching extreme_action adds 1. A tick then adds
sqrt(0.03333 * 0.5) * randfn() * volatility plus effective trend. Prices
are clamped to nominal min/max only when bankrupt is zero, otherwise to
$0.87-$10,000,000. Once LEVELS_UNLOCKED exceeds bankrupt, stored trend
falls by 0.01 every tick; otherwise it reverses near an outward-facing
nominal boundary. Equal nominal minimum and maximum force a fixed price.
The temporary progression bonuses do not rewrite stored trend. Each asset
keeps a 100-price graph history in memory.

Mission events and implant prices
---------------------------------
The first successful completion of each eligible built-in campaign mission
realizes its authored stock impacts once in that save slot. Missions can move
more than two shares; Darkworld slightly lowers only CRUS. Every built-in
mission except House has an event. The stock screen's Market Outlook describes each mission's expected effects and marks rising and falling
tickers without disclosing exact percentages. The latest completed mission's
authored percentage impacts appear in one line beside the Outlook heading
only while their movement is active. Rising and falling forecast tickers occupy
separate lines; the two market-wide forecasts list every company ticker on its
own line. Secret mission forecasts remain hidden until those missions
are unlocked. The Headquarters forecast requires Hope Eradicated and Death
mode to have been active together at least once in that save slot. Replacing
Cruelty Squad's CEO lifts CRUS sharply and hurts rival SCRD. When none
are available, the panel says so. Ordinary impacts are spread across 20 seconds and start a
matching growth or decline period. Other market movement continues during
that period, so the final observed price need not differ by exactly the
authored impact percentage. Replaying a mission does not repeat its effects.

Apartment Atrocity's GAGA -100% is a special terminal event. Its price falls
linearly from its value at mission completion to zero over 20 seconds, then
remains at zero until Archon Grid restores it at its original listing price
and gives it the same 99% upward impact as every other company stock. Purchases
are blocked while it is bankrupt, and existing shares retain their quantity. The other negative
events use the ordinary percentage movement and can recover later. Trauma
Loop applies a 99% decline to every company stock; its event can go below the
ordinary market price floor. Fish and body parts are unaffected by these two
market-wide events. Stock
event version 3 clears the one-time history from earlier outlook versions,
allowing an existing save to replay missions and receive the revised events.
The complete event list is in market_outlook_events.txt.

The implant shop quotes head items against Pure Optics, torso items against
Godhead Heavy Industries, leg items against G-Tech, and arm items against
Security Redefined. Other priced implants use Consumer Softproducts. The
implant tooltip shows a live price and linked ticker on one line. The multiplier is 1 + 0.15 * natural_log(current company price / starting price),
clamped to 0.75-1.35. Zero-price items remain free. The quote is captured
when the confirmation opens, so a tick during confirmation does not silently
change the amount charged.

Finding fish and parts
----------------------
Each level has a POSSIBLE_FISH ticker pool in Stock_Handler.gd; toilets use a
separate BNBO-only pool. Once a fishing hook is in water or has collided, it
attempts to produce a fish every second while none is caught. Each attempt
first passes a 50% random gate, then picks uniformly from that level's pool.
For the selected fish, another 0–100 roll must be no greater than fish_chance
plus 0.1 times the hook's capped distance from the player (0–50), plus 5 in
rain and 3.5 if the fishing-bonus head implant is worn. Some fish also require
night (the computer's local clock before 06:00 or after 21:00), rain, or Hope
Eradicated. A bite remains
available for fish_speed + distance * 0.002 seconds before timing out. Reeling
the caught fish back records its ticker as found and adds it to the holding.

These are code-level rolls, not a guaranteed per-second catch probability:
the selected level pool can repeat a ticker, the first gate can fail, and
conditions can reject the selected fish. Body-part pickups use their part
name, add one unit to that asset, mark it found, and hide the pickup for the
session. The corresponding fish/part discovery counts in Statistics and Goals
read these found lists rather than how many units are currently owned.

Saving, slots, and online sessions
----------------------------------
Market data is stored separately from campaign cash. Slot 1 uses
user://stocks.save; slots 2–4 use
user://crus_online_slots/slotN/stocks.save. Each stock entry saves its ticker,
owned quantity, current price, previous price, stored trend, company market
state, its remaining duration, and any pending mission movement; the file also contains fish_found,
org_found, and the list of market events already realized. On load, price,
owned, trend, company state, and completed market events are
restored. Previous price is written to disk but is not currently restored by
load_stocks(), and the last-100-point graph is not stored. A missing or empty
stock save is initialized from catalogue defaults or the stock backup.

Trades save stock data and campaign cash immediately. Other menu transitions
and mission starts save stock data, and the game's two-minute backup saves a
stock_backup.save alongside its campaign backup. Switching save slots resets
the market to initial values before loading that slot's own data. Clearing
save data resets stock prices, trends, holdings, and discovery lists. During a
temporary non-Cruelty mode, CampaignSave snapshots market state, suppresses
normal stock writes, and restores the campaign state on return. Stock_Handler
contains no network price or transaction synchronization; the market is local
to each player's save rather than a shared lobby order book.

Current persistence detail: the base fishing hook calls save_stocks() after
marking a fish found but BEFORE increasing its owned quantity. The discovery
therefore persists immediately, while that newly caught unit requires a later
stock save to persist. Body-part pickups save after adding their unit.
